Privacy Policy

Applies to Night Shift Radio and other games published by Shrimptown Games. Last updated October 1, 2026.

The short version

  • We don't ask for your name, email, or any account.
  • Your game progress is saved on your own device or in your own browser.
  • The mobile version shows ads through Google AdMob, and the browser versions on game portals show ads through that portal. Those ad services collect some device information, described below.
  • We don't sell your information.

What the game stores

Your progress (money, upgrades, voicemails found, settings) is saved on your device. On Windows it lives in your AppData folder. On Android it lives in the app's private storage. In a web browser it lives in that browser's site storage. Deleting the game, or clearing the browser's site data, deletes it. We never receive a copy.

Time check

The Windows and Android versions ask a public web server for the current date and time when they start, so offline earnings can't be inflated by changing the device clock. This request sends no information about you beyond what any web request includes (such as your IP address), and we don't keep it.

Ads on Android (Google AdMob)

The Android version uses Google AdMob to show ads: optional ads you choose to watch for a reward, short full-screen ads at natural breaks, and a banner on the title screen. AdMob may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers, including your device's advertising ID
  • Approximate location based on your IP address
  • How you interact with ads, and app diagnostics such as crash logs and performance data

Google uses this to show ads, measure them, and prevent fraud. Players in the European Economic Area, the UK, and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and can change that choice later. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Google's practices are described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Browser versions on game portals

When you play on a game portal such as CrazyGames or Poki, the portal shows the ads and may use cookies and similar technology. The portal's own privacy policy applies to that: CrazyGames privacy policy, Poki privacy policy. The version on itch.io shows no ads.

Purchases

The Android version offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play, and other versions may be sold through stores such as Steam or itch.io. Payment is handled entirely by that store. We never see your card details. Google Play tells the game which items you own so it can unlock them.

Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information through one of our games, contact us and we'll help remove it.

Your choices

  • Opt out of personalized ads on Android in your device settings, or through the consent form shown in the game.
  • Delete your progress by uninstalling the game or clearing browser site data.
  • Ask us anything about your data at the email below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
